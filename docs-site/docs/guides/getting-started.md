# Getting Started

This guide walks you through setting up your Halo account and creating your first lending circle.

---

## Prerequisites

- A **Google** or **GitHub** account (for authentication)
- A **Stacks wallet** — [Leather](https://leather.io/) or [Xverse](https://www.xverse.app/)
- Some **STX** for transaction fees (get free testnet STX from the [Stacks faucet](https://explorer.hiro.so/sandbox/faucet))

---

## Step 1: Sign In

1. Go to [gethalo.fun](https://gethalo.fun)
2. Click **Get Started**
3. Sign in with your Google or GitHub account

---

## Step 2: Connect Your Wallet

1. After signing in, you'll be prompted to connect your Stacks wallet
2. Click **Connect Wallet**
3. Choose Leather or Xverse
4. Approve the connection in your wallet extension

---

## Step 3: Bind Your Identity

This is a one-time step that permanently links your social account to your wallet address.

1. Click **Bind Wallet** on the connect-wallet page
2. Your wallet will prompt you to sign a transaction
3. Approve the transaction (costs a small STX fee)
4. Wait for the transaction to confirm (~10 minutes on testnet)

> **Note**: This binding is permanent and one-to-one. You cannot change your wallet later, and this wallet cannot be bound to another account.

---

## Step 4: Get Test Tokens

On testnet, you can get free tokens from the Halo faucet:

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Click **Get Test Tokens** or visit the faucet directly
3. You'll receive:
   - **1,000 hUSD** (test stablecoin)
   - **0.01 sBTC** (test Bitcoin wrapper)
4. Rate limit: once per 24 hours

---

## Step 5: Deposit Collateral

Before joining circles, you need collateral in the vault:

1. Go to the **Vault** page
2. Select an asset to deposit (hUSD, STX, or sBTC)
3. Enter the amount
4. Click **Deposit** and approve the transaction
5. Your collateral capacity will update after confirmation

---

## Step 6: Create or Join a Circle

### Create a Circle

1. Go to **Circles** → **Create Circle**
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Name**: Give your circle a name
   - **Contribution**: How much each member contributes per round
   - **Members**: How many members (3–10)
   - **Round Duration**: How long each round lasts
   - **Grace Period**: Time to contribute before being marked late
3. Click **Create** and approve the transaction
4. Share the **invite link** with friends

### Join a Circle

1. Click an invite link shared by a circle creator
2. Or browse the **Explore** page for open circles
3. Click **Join** and approve the transaction
4. The circle starts automatically when full

---

## Step 7: Participate

Once the circle is active:

1. **Each round**, contribute your fixed amount before the deadline
2. On-time payments build your **credit score**
3. When it's your turn, you receive the **full pool payout** (minus 1% fee)
4. After all rounds complete, your collateral is released
