# Halo Protocol

**Decentralized lending circles built on Stacks, secured by Bitcoin.**

Halo brings the ancient practice of community savings circles (ROSCAs) on-chain — giving underbanked communities access to transparent, trustless group lending with on-chain credit scoring, collateral vaults, and sBTC staking.

---

## What is Halo?

Halo Protocol is a decentralized finance (DeFi) protocol that enables **peer-to-peer lending circles** on the [Stacks](https://www.stacks.co/) blockchain. Members pool funds each round, and one member receives the full pot — rotating until everyone has been paid out. Smart contracts enforce fairness, collateral protects against defaults, and an on-chain credit score rewards reliable participation.

Think of it as **a community savings group with the trust guarantees of Bitcoin**.

---

## Why Halo?

| Problem | Halo's Solution |
|---|---|
| Traditional ROSCAs rely on social trust and have no enforcement | Smart contracts enforce contributions, payouts, and collateral |
| No credit history for underbanked populations | On-chain credit scoring (300–850) built from verifiable behavior |
| Default risk destroys the circle | Collateral vault with LTV enforcement — defaults are covered |
| Opaque fund management | Every transaction is on-chain and auditable |
| Limited to local communities | Global circles with invite links, no geography restrictions |

## Core Concepts

### Lending Circles (ROSCA)
A group of 3–10 members each contribute a fixed amount per round. Each round, one member receives the full pool. The cycle continues until every member has received a payout. [Learn more →](/protocol/circles)

### Credit Scoring
Every payment, completion, and staking activity builds your on-chain credit score. Higher scores unlock better circles and lower collateral requirements. [Learn more →](/protocol/credit-scoring)

### Collateral Vault
Members deposit collateral (hUSD, STX, or sBTC) to back their circle commitments. The vault enforces loan-to-value ratios and slashes collateral on default. [Learn more →](/protocol/vault)

### sBTC Staking
Stake sBTC to earn yield and boost your credit score. Longer locks and larger stakes earn higher rewards. [Learn more →](/protocol/sbtc-staking)
