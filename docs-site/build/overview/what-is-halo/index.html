What is Halo?

Halo Protocol is a decentralized lending circle platform built on the Stacks blockchain and secured by Bitcoin. It brings the ancient practice of community savings groups on-chain, adding trustless smart contract enforcement, collateral protection, and portable credit scoring.

The Problem​

2 billion people worldwide lack access to traditional banking and credit systems. Yet many of these communities have been running informal savings groups — known as ROSCAs, chit funds, tandas, susus, or stokvels — for generations.

These informal groups face critical challenges:

  • Trust failures: A member can take their payout and disappear
  • No enforcement: There's no mechanism to compel contributions
  • No credit building: Years of reliable participation create no credit history
  • Geographic limits: Groups are restricted to local communities
  • Opacity: Fund management is often informal and undocumented

The Solution​

Halo digitizes and decentralizes lending circles with:

Smart Contract Enforcement​

Every contribution, payout, and rule is enforced by immutable smart contracts on Stacks. No one can cheat, skip, or modify the terms after creation.

Collateral Protection​

Members deposit collateral in a multi-asset vault. If someone defaults, their collateral is slashed to cover the gap. Other members are always protected.

On-Chain Credit Scoring​

Every on-time payment, completed circle, and staking activity builds a verifiable, portable credit score (300–850). This score lives on-chain and can be used across protocols.

Global Access​

Anyone with a Stacks wallet and an internet connection can join. No banks, no paperwork, no geography restrictions. Just connect, deposit collateral, and join a circle.

Bitcoin Security​

Built on Stacks, Halo transactions settle on Bitcoin — the most secure blockchain in the world. Clarity smart contracts are decidable, preventing entire classes of exploits.

Who Is Halo For?​

Underbanked Communities​

People who participate in informal savings groups but lack access to formal credit. Halo lets them build verifiable credit history.

Crypto-Native Savers​

DeFi users who want structured savings vehicles beyond lending/borrowing protocols. Circles provide accountability and community.

Diaspora Communities​

People sending money home or coordinating savings across borders. Halo removes geographic barriers.

DAOs and Teams​

Organizations that want to create structured savings pools for their members with transparent, on-chain accounting.

Core Primitives​

Halo is composed of four interlocking primitives:

  1. Identity — Sybil-resistant wallet binding via social auth
  2. Circles — The lending circle engine (classic ROSCA + bidding)
  3. Credit — On-chain credit scoring from verifiable behavior
  4. Vault — Multi-asset collateral with LTV enforcement and yield

Each primitive is a standalone smart contract that other protocols can integrate with. Your Halo credit score, for example, can be read by any Stacks contract.

The Vision​

Halo aims to be the credit layer for Bitcoin DeFi. By creating verifiable credit histories from real economic behavior, we're building the foundation for:

  • Under-collateralized lending — Excellent credit scores could reduce collateral requirements
  • Cross-protocol reputation — Your Halo score is portable across the Stacks ecosystem
  • Financial inclusion — Building credit without a bank account
  • Community-driven finance — Groups helping each other, enforced by code
